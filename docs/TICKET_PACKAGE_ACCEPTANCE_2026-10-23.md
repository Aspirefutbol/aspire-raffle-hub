## Pricing architecture delta

This section supersedes older single-price-only statements in `docs/architecture.md` and historical records. The approved sale amount is no longer always quantity multiplied by the single-ticket price.

Campaigns retain the required single-ticket price plus optional ticket packages. Validation rejects duplicate quantities, malformed or nonpositive prices, fractional cents/quantities and unsupported limits. The quote selects the lowest-cost exact combination of configured packages for a purchase, with deterministic tie handling. Packages do not combine buyers or prior transactions.

The database is authoritative. It computes the locked campaign quote and rejects a supplied amount that differs. The browser uses the matching calculation for preview and confirmation, never as a replacement for server enforcement. Prices and package disclosures lock at campaign activation.

New sales retain an immutable pricing snapshot and exact per-ticket integer-cent allocations. Five tickets at $90 allocate $18 each; six at $110 allocate five at $18 and one at $20. Receipt allocations and drawing face-value totals agree with the recorded sale total. Each eligible issued ticket remains one entry, regardless of its allocated price.

Original sale, ledger, audit and drawing rows are not rewritten. Legacy sales without package snapshots retain their original single-price behavior. Reversals append events and retire old ticket numbers. Linked corrections issue fresh numbers. Explicit credit transfers use money already held and do not manufacture another receipt. Reconciliation and returned-check handling remain separate from ticket validity.

Tenant scope, guardian/seller authority, campaign locks, caller-bound idempotency, safe retries after uncertain responses, stock limits and immutable audit chains are retained. Public pages disclose offers without exposing private family/buyer information and remain request-only.
